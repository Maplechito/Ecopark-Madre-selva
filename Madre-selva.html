Who We Are

A Refuge Born from Urgency

Madre Selva is a nature reserve in Bonfil, Cancún. It is managed by Equilibrium Wayak. They are a local group. They save native plants. They grow trees. They protect animals. This place is not a zoo. It is not a theme park. It is a green home for many living things.

Here you can find ceibas, mahogany, and medicinal herbs. Volunteers come every week. They plant seeds. They clean the paths. They watch the forest grow. The forest is a teacher. It shows us how to live with nature.

Every tree is important. Every flower is a victory. The concrete city is very close. But the forest is still here. It needs our help. It needs your help.

The Threat

What We're Fighting

Madre Selva exists because Cancún is disappearing. Buildings and roads take the land. Three big problems are very close. We must know them. We must stop them.

🏗️

Industrial Deforestation

Big companies build warehouses. They cut many trees. The jungle is very old. It took thousands of years to grow. In one day, machines can destroy it. This is happening now. We are losing green areas very fast.

🗑️

Clandestine Dumping

Some people throw trash in secret places. They leave plastic, chemicals, and old furniture. This trash goes into the ground. It reaches the water. The same water we drink. It is very dangerous for everyone.

💧

Aquifer Contamination

Under our feet there is limestone rock. It has many holes. Water passes through easily. Oil, soap, and poisons go straight down. They contaminate the underground rivers. The cenotes become sick. The animals suffer.

Take Action

How You Can Help

You don't need money or expertise. You need to care enough to act.

  • 01

    Leave No Trace

    Never dump waste near the reserve. The limestone filters nothing. Trash becomes poison for the water.

  • 02

    Volunteer Your Time

    Join a weekend reforestation day. Help the beekeepers. Catalogue the birds and insects.

  • 03

    Buy a Native Tree

    Purchase a ceiba or mahogany seedling. Plant it at home or donate it. Every tree is a permanent act.

  • 04

    Spread the Word

    Share this page. Bring a friend. Keep the conversation alive. The forest needs voices.
